
# **ESERCIZIO 1**

A) Linguaggio stringhe $\{a^nb^{2n} \quad t.c. \quad n\geq 0\}$

$$
G = (\{S\},\{a,b,\epsilon\},P,S)
$$

$$
P = \{S\rightarrow\epsilon \mid aSbb\}
$$



---
B) Linguaggio stringhe $\{ (abc)^n (cba)^n\quad t.c. \quad n\geq 0\}$

$$
G = (\{S\},\{a,b,c,\epsilon\},P,S)
$$
$$
P = \{S\rightarrow\epsilon\mid abcScba\}
$$

---
C) Linguaggio stringhe $\{ (a)^n (b)^m \quad t.c. \quad n\leq m\}$
$$
G = (\{S\},\{a,b,\epsilon\},P,S)
$$
$$
P = \{S\rightarrow\epsilon \mid aSb \mid Sb\}
$$
---
D) Linguaggio stringhe $\{ (a)^n (b)^m (c)^n \quad t.c. \quad  \text{n pari e m dispari} \}$
$$
G = (\{S, E\},\{a,b,c\},P,S)
$$
$$P = \left\{ 
\begin{aligned}
 S &\rightarrow aaScc \mid E \\ 
 E &\rightarrow b \mid bEb
\end{aligned} \right\}$$
---
E) Linguaggio stringhe $\{ (a)^n (b)^m (c)^p \quad t.c. \quad n \leq m+p \}$
$$
G = (\{S,E\},\{a,b,c,\epsilon\},P,S)
$$
$$P = \left\{ 
\begin{aligned}
S &\rightarrow aSc \mid Sc \mid E \\ 
E &\rightarrow aEb \mid Eb \mid \epsilon \\ 
\end{aligned} \right\}$$

# Per casa

$S \rightarrow SS \mid (S) \mid ()$

Possiamo dimostrare che questa grammatica è ambigua prendendo come esempio la stringa 
"()()()".

- $S \Rightarrow SS \Rightarrow SSS \Rightarrow ()SS \Rightarrow ()()S \Rightarrow ()()()$
- $S \Rightarrow SS \Rightarrow ()S \Rightarrow ()SS \Rightarrow ()()S \Rightarrow ()()()$

```
Primo Albero
	   S
      / \
     S   S
    / \   \
   S   S   ()
  /     \
 ()     ()
 
Secondo albero
       S
      / \
     S   S
    /   / \
   ()  S   S
      /     \
     ()     ()

```

- $S \Rightarrow SS$$\Rightarrow SSS$$\Rightarrow ()SS$$\Rightarrow ()()S$$\Rightarrow ()()()$
- $S \Rightarrow SS$$\Rightarrow ()S$$\Rightarrow ()SS$$\Rightarrow ()()S$$\Rightarrow ()()()$

Derivazioni canoniche destre:

- $S \Rightarrow SS$$\Rightarrow S()$$\Rightarrow SS()$$\Rightarrow S()()$$\Rightarrow ()()()$
